Browser Bible

Bible study application with Greek and Hebrew. Free, no account required — works in any modern browser.

Developed by the Digital Bible Society with major contributions from John Dyer and Michael Johnson. Audio by Faith Comes by Hearing, video by Jesus Film Project. Source code: GitHub.

If you would like to help keep this site operating and expanding to reach more people with more Bible translations in more languages, you can donate to help support what it really costs so that we can keep offering the Word of God for free. No cost to the end user means no barriers to getting the life-saving message of the Good News of Jesus Christ, and more people who will find eternal life in Him.
