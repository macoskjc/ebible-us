World English Bible

The World English Bible (WEB) is a free, public domain translation of the Holy Bible — modern English, accurate, and freely shareable. No copyright restrictions. Read, download, print, or share without asking permission.

Editions

WEBU

World English Bible Updated

The current, actively-maintained edition. Uses "LORD" instead of "Yahweh." U.S.A. spelling. Old and New Testaments.

Read ePub Details
WEB / WEBUS

World English Bible Classic

The original edition using "Yahweh" for the divine name. U.S.A. spelling. Includes Old Testament, New Testament, and Deuterocanon.

Read ePub Details
WEBBE

World English Bible British Edition

British/International spelling ("colour," "honour," etc.) with Deuterocanon. Uses "LORD."

Read ePub Details
WEBP

World English Bible Protestant

66-book Protestant canon only (no Deuterocanon). U.S.A. spelling, "LORD."

Read ePub Details
WEBC

World English Bible Catholic

Catholic canon arrangement. Includes Deuterocanonical books placed in traditional Catholic order.

Read ePub Details
WMB / WMBB

World Messianic Bible

Uses Hebrew names (Yeshua, Adonai, etc.). Available in U.S.A. and British editions.
