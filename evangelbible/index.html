Our Work

Bible Translation

We provide Scripture in mother-tongue languages to communities that have never had access to God's Word in their own language. Every person deserves to hear the Gospel in the language of their heart.

Literacy

Giving people access to God's life-changing Word often involves language development. We work to equip people with skills to read and write in their mother tongue language.

Church Planting

We serve the under-resourced church globally through Biblical resources and training, with the goal to transform lives worldwide through God's Word. Equipped with the Word, churches take root.

Relief & Development

We are devoted to human flourishing. We work to improve the lives and livelihoods of vulnerable people, building lasting relationships and increasing self-sufficiency.

Translator Teams

Our translator teams are made up of mother-tongue speakers trained in Bible translation principles — ensuring that Scripture is accurate, clear, and natural in every language we serve. Meet the teams →
